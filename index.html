"I design games where the systems tell the story. The world doesn't exist until you scan it into memory."

I am a system designer who bridges the gap between design philosophy and technical execution. I design mechanics by analyzing every interdependency, untangling convoluted systems until I have connected every single dot, together with the whole. Everything I design, I can also build. I work in Unreal Engine and C++, and I move freely between a design document and the engine that runs it. My experience includes leading teams from concept to release, porting shipped titles to Nintendo Switch 1 and 2, and winning awards for my university work. Right now, I am aiming to create even bigger and better things.
