Concept Mockups

Three ways to show the work

Same deep-sea theme, same fake content. Three different layouts. Open each, get a feel, and tell me which one to build for real (or which bits to mix). The theme lives in the atmosphere and the styling, never in the navigation.

01

The Descent Scroll

One ordinary page. Scrolling down IS descending: the water darkens, the depth gauge climbs, sections bioluminesce in. Surface = you, mid-water = the work, seabed = your origins. Most legible, most on-theme.

The Observatory

A fixed console. Your bio on the left, a menu (Work / Traits / Lineage), and a porthole that loads each clean view in place. Feels like operating a deep-sea research station. Deliberate, no scrolling.

The Specimen Archive
