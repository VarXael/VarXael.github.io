Abyssal
Constellations
#ui-design #fl0w
Unlike rigid grid systems or standard node networks, an abyssal constellation relies on free-floating anchor points suspended in a high-viscosity void.
Connections are not rendered as strict linear paths, but rather as sweeping quadratic bezier curves. This injects organic tension into the data lattice, mimicking the delicate, filamentous structures of deep-sea micro-organisms.
