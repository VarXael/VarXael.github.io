fl0w Mechanics

#game-design #simulation

The core concept revolves around mapping organic movement not through rigid skeletal animation, but through mathematical arrays preserving historical coordinate data.

By applying a high friction coefficient and drawing bezier curves through the historical points, we simulate a high-viscosity fluid environment.
