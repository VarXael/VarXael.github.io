Bezier Sweeps & Curves

#geometry #fl0w

Unlike radial systems which depend on strict rotational symmetry, a constellation relies on free-floating anchor points.

To prevent the network from looking rigid or mechanical, connections are rendered using quadratic bezier curves, where the control point is offset by time. This creates a sweeping, organic tension between the data nodes.
