Micro-Geometric Skeletons

Crystalline Exoskeletons

Focused on the mathematical beauty of microscopic life. Designed procedural generation systems that build complex, symmetrical crystalline shells for entities based on their consumption history.

Radial Movement

Unlike segmented swimmers, these geometric organisms utilize rotational momentum. By absorbing nutrients, they spin their outer rings, generating microscopic currents to pull in prey.

Bioluminescent Cores

Integrated deep-sea lighting mechanics. The entity's core acts as a localized light source, casting complex geometric shadows onto the ocean floor based on the intricate patterns of its outer shell.
